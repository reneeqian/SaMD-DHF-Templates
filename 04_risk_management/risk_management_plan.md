# Risk Management Plan — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

## Scope

This plan covers software-related risks for {{PROJECT_NAME}} per ISO 14971:2019.

## Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Software Developer | Identify and implement risk controls (RSK requirements) |
| Reviewer | Verify risk controls are tested and traceable |

## Risk Acceptability Criteria

| Severity | Probability | Acceptability |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Negligible | Any | Acceptable |
| Minor | Remote or less | Acceptable |
| Serious | Occasional or less | ALARP review required |
| Critical | Remote or less | Residual risk evaluation required |
| Catastrophic | Any | Unacceptable without specific mitigation |

## Review Schedule

Risk management records shall be reviewed at each major release.
